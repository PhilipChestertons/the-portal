A charming end of terrace three bedroom house enviably located in close proximity to Kings Cross. Full of delightful period charm, it offers two receptions, three bedrooms, two bathrooms and plenty of outside space in the form a wonderful roof terrace and a good sized rear garden. The latter is accessed via both the kitchen and the reception. Being sold chain-free.

It is ideally located in close proximity to Kings Cross and the fantastic Granary Square which boasts a vast array of shops and cafes/restaurants. Transport links are superb with Kings Cross and St Pancras nearby. This property is surrounded by outstanding schools such as Camden School for Girls, Cavendish School, Northbridge House, Hawley Infant School and St Mary Magdalene Academy.
